Move your delivery by asking for it.

FedE is the FedEx assistant that does the work. Say when and where you want a package, and it reschedules, holds, or redirects the delivery itself, then keeps watching until it arrives.

Three things it does for you

Reschedules a delivery

Pick a day in plain words. FedE checks the weather at your address first and steers you to a day that will actually work.

"Reschedule FX100001 to Friday"

Holds or redirects a package

Not going to be home? FedE holds it at the nearest FedEx location or sends it to another address. Redirects and cancellations always ask you to confirm first.

"Hold FX100006 for pickup"

Watches it until it lands

Tell FedE when you need something and it keeps an eye on delays, storms, and failed attempts, then warns you and offers the fix before you have to ask.

"I need FX100001 before Friday"

How it works

  1. 1

    You ask

    Type it or say it. "Where is my package", "move it to Monday", "send it to my office".

  2. 2

    FedE plans

    It reads your shipments, checks the route and the weather, and decides which actions get you there.

  3. 3

    FedE acts, within your permissions

    Each action is checked against your account and FedEx rules before anything changes. Big changes wait for your confirmation.

  4. 4

    You see what happened

    A plain confirmation in the chat, the new plan on the map, and a record of every action under Updates.

Built so the agent cannot overstep

FedE can change real deliveries, so every control sits in code the model cannot talk its way around.

Your account, your packages
Sign-in issues a short-lived signed token. Every tool checks that the package belongs to you before it acts, so a customer can never touch someone else's shipment.
Big changes wait for you
Redirects and cancellations are refused by the tool itself until you confirm in a pop-up, even if the model is tricked into trying.
No change is claimed unless it is real
Every change is read back from FedEx systems before FedE says it is done. A reply that claims a change with no action behind it is caught and corrected.
One rules engine
The chat agent and the buttons on screen call the same operations layer, so permissions and business rules can never drift apart.
Your microphone stays yours
Voice asks for consent every time, turns the mic off on its own, and deletes audio right after it is transcribed.
Hardened edges
Strict content security policy, allow-listed origins, rate limits, input validation, and sessions that end on their own. Code is scanned on every push.
