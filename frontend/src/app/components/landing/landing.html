What makes FedE different

Not a chatbot that hands you links. An agent that plans, acts and confirms.

🤖

Agentic planner

Claude decides which tools to call and in what order: track, list, reschedule, redirect, cancel, notifications. Multi-step plans, real database mutations.

🎤

Voice assistant

Hands-free support with speech in and speech out. It waits for you to finish speaking before responding, with explicit microphone consent.

🔒

Secure by design

OAuth2 + JWT sign-in, role-based authorization inside every tool, CORS allow-list, rate limiting, security headers and fail-closed secrets.

⚡

Proactive, not reactive

Delay or exception on your package? FedE surfaces it and offers the fix, reschedule or redirect, in the same reply.

🧭

One rules engine

The agent's tools and the REST API share the same operations layer, so business rules and permissions can never drift apart.

🛡️

Graceful degradation

No AI key? The platform falls back to a deterministic tracker so core support never goes down with the model.

How it works

From a sentence to a completed action in four steps.

1

Sign in

OAuth2 password flow issues a short-lived JWT. Your role (customer or agent) travels with every request.

2

Ask naturally

Type or speak: "where are my packages?", "move FX100001 to Friday", "send it to my office instead".

3

The agent plans

Claude reads the conversation, picks the right tools with the right arguments, and loops until the job is done.

4

Verified action

Every tool call passes authorization and business rules before touching the database. You get a plain-language confirmation.
